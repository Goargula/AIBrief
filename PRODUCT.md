# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Readers who want a fast, mobile-first briefing on important AI news, research, funding, policy, infrastructure, and adoption stories. They may arrive from the homepage, a shared story link, search, or a saved story.

## Product Purpose

AI Brief turns a curated AI news feed into a swipeable story reader. Success means a reader can understand the most important development quickly, open source coverage when needed, save stories locally, and continue through the feed without friction.

## Brand Personality

Sharp, practical, editorially restrained.

## Anti-references

Avoid marketing-site theatrics, decorative motion, generic AI-generated news copy, and interactions that make the user wait before reaching the story they requested.

## Design Principles

- Put the requested story first when intent is explicit.
- Keep the reader in the flow of the feed after each story.
- Prefer immediate, stable loading over animated choreography.
- Preserve editorial trust through clear source links and story-specific context.
- Keep controls familiar and quiet so the story remains the focus.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Respect reduced-motion preferences, avoid page-load motion that delays content, keep interactive controls keyboard-accessible, and preserve readable contrast over story visuals.
