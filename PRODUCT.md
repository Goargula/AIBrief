# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People who want a fast, curated view of important AI news, research, funding, policy, and industry developments without reading many separate publications.

## Product Purpose

AI Brief is a personal intelligence reader that turns a broad curated feed into a continuous, mobile-first story deck. Success means readers can quickly understand what changed, why it matters, and which stories are new since the last refresh.

## Brand Personality

Direct, informed, concise.

## Anti-references

Avoid noisy news portals, generic link aggregators, opaque ranking, and interfaces that hide feed changes or make readers guess what is new.

## Design Principles

- Make freshness and refresh deltas explicit.
- Keep high-signal context readable without leaving the reader.
- Preserve a fast, familiar swipe-through workflow.
- Use ranking to prioritize, not to hide coverage.
- Keep controls compact and their state obvious.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain strong text contrast, keyboard navigation, semantic controls, readable touch targets, and reduced-motion-safe interactions.
